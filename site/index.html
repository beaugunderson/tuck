A little less menu bar.

I got annoyed with Bartender, so I made this. Click the chevron to see your hidden menu bar icons, then click an icon to open it.

Download for MacFree forever.

macOS 15+ · Signed & notarized

About 14 MB of memory

Around 0% idle CPU in local testing. Written in Swift, with no always-on mouse tracking.

Remembers your layout

Right-click to choose which icons stay visible. Tuck keeps separate layouts for different screen widths.

Install Tuck

Download

Download, unzip, and move Tuck to Applications. It asks once whether to keep itself updated.

Get the latest Tuck ↓

Or use Homebrew

brew install --cask beaugunderson/tap/tuck

Tuck needs Accessibility to move and click icons, and Screen Recording to show their glyphs. On macOS 27 it uses Full Disk Access instead of Screen Recording, to change which apps macOS allows in the menu bar. If the icons don’t appear after granting permission, restart Tuck from its right-click menu.

Thanks, Ice ↗.

Tuck’s menu bar clicking and moving code is adapted from Ice, by Jordan Baird and its contributors. Its capture technique and Ice Bar are the basis for Tuck’s hidden-icon strip.
