01 / NEAT

Out of sight.
One click away.

Click the chevron to see your hidden icons. Click an icon to open it. The real glyphs, in their original colors, on a background that fits your menu bar.

02 / TINY

Light on your Mac.
And your mind.

Native Swift. No always-on mouse tracking. Around 0% idle CPU and 14 MB of memory in local testing. It manages your menu bar, not your whole life.

Choose what stays visible with a right-click. Tuck remembers your layout for different screen widths, from the laptop to the big monitor.

Get the latest Tuck ↓

For the terminally inclined

HOMEBREW
brew install --cask beaugunderson/tap/tuck

One small setup step. Allow Accessibility so Tuck can move and click icons, and Screen Recording so it can show their actual glyphs. If they don’t appear right away, restart Tuck from its right-click menu.

One universal download for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Prefer to build it yourself? The source is all yours.

A tip of the hat to Ice ↗.

Tuck’s core menu bar clicking and moving code is adapted from Ice, the open-source menu bar manager by Jordan Baird and its contributors. Its icon capture approach and Ice Bar also helped make Tuck’s hidden-icon strip possible. Thank you for sharing the work.

Both projects are free software, licensed under GPLv3.
